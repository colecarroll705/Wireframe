Etch-A-Sketch User Guide

What is an Etch-A-Sketch?

Etch A Sketch is a mechanical drawing toy created by André Cassagnes. They have thick, flat gray screens surrounded by a red plastic frame. On the front of the frame are two white knobs in the lower corners. Twisting these knobs is the main mechanism and causes aluminum powder to be displaced on the back of the screen, leaving a solid line.
